# CULTURAL DESCENDANT 001

**Status:** bounded executable R10 witness  
**Date:** 2026-10-01

## Thesis

An admitted local consequence may be taken up, varied, reproduced, and crossed onward without rewriting its ancestor or inheriting the ancestor's authority.

The MEMENTO donor law is:

> **Correct with descendants and supersession; do not rewrite old testimony to make the present look inevitable.**

R10 translates that into protocol machinery:

```text
ANCESTOR CROSSING X
       ↓
LOCAL RECEIVE
       ↓
LOCAL ADMIT
       ↓
FIELD PROJECTION
       ↓
OWNER-LOCAL UPTAKE U
       ↓
EXPLICIT VARIATION
       ↓
FRESH DESCENDANT CROSSING Y
       ↓
ANOTHER WORLD
       ↓
RECEIVED AS FRESH CANDIDATE
```

## Cultural uptake

A Cultural Uptake is a content-addressed local occurrence.

It binds:

- ancestor crossing ID;
- local R3 ADMIT receipt ID;
- R9 field projection ID;
- R9 field history root;
- local world and particular;
- explicit variation;
- local note;
- explicit non-inheritance of authority.

The field is context, not cause or authority.

```text
FIELD != CAUSE
FIELD != AUTHORITY
UPTAKE != AUTOMATION
```

A valid uptake cannot be created from weather alone.

It requires a real verified ancestor crossing and a real signed local R3 ADMIT receipt already represented in the field history.

## Explicit variation

Variation is structurally divided into four lists:

```text
preserved
varied
introduced
retired
```

At least one `varied` statement is required.

This prevents a descendant from claiming ancestry while leaving mutation implicit.

Example:

```text
preserved:
  - return-after-transformation
  - attributable ancestry

varied:
  - return becomes an invitation rather than closure

introduced:
  - second-world reply door

retired:
  - single-world ending assumption
```

The uptake itself is content-addressed, so rewriting the declared variation changes its identity.

```text
VARIATION != RETCON
```

## Descendant crossing

The descendant is not a mutation of the ancestor crossing.

It is a new signed crossing with:

```text
parents = [ancestor_crossing_id]
source_world = local uptake world
source_particular = local maker
source_history_head = field history root
declared_kind = R10_CULTURAL_DESCENDANT
```

Its requested effect is:

```text
kind = fresh-candidate-local-uptake
authority = receiver-local
```

The lineage extension binds:

- uptake ID;
- ancestor crossing ID;
- ancestor ADMIT receipt ID;
- field projection ID;
- field history root;
- explicit variation.

It also fixes:

```text
inherited_authority = false
field_authorized_action = false
```

## Fresh authority

The executable specimen signs the descendant with a fresh local key.

The ancestor source remains attributable, but the ancestor's signature does not authorize the descendant.

The field projection also does not authorize the descendant.

The descendant exists because a local owner performs a fresh act.

```text
ANCESTRY != AUTHORITY
DESCENDANT != ANCESTOR
REPRODUCTION != AUTHORIZATION
```

## Fresh candidate in another world

The decisive R10 crossing sends descendant Y to another durable Local Receiver.

The next world emits:

```text
kind = RECEIVED
semantic_effect = none
```

and has no disposition yet.

The next world does not inherit:

- the ancestor world's ADMIT;
- the descendant-producing world's ADMIT;
- the R9 field's susceptibility;
- the ancestor's source authority.

It receives Y as a fresh candidate under its own law.

```text
ANCESTOR ADMIT != DESCENDANT ADMIT
REPRODUCTION != ADMISSION
ARRIVAL != INHERITED CANON
```

## Append-only lineage

Changing the ancestor does not edit the descendant.

Changing the uptake variation after addressing invalidates the uptake.

Changing the descendant parent after signing invalidates the descendant signature.

Lineage therefore grows by new addressed occurrences rather than by rewriting old ones.

```text
OLD TESTIMONY != CURRENT INTERPRETATION
CORRECTION -> DESCENDANT
MUTATION -> DESCENDANT
```

## What this earns

At the current envelope level, this satisfies R10:

- descendant preserves ancestry;
- descendant does not inherit source authority;
- variation is explicit and identity-bearing;
- field consequence is context, not authorization;
- descendant is a fresh signed crossing;
- another sovereign world receives the descendant as a fresh candidate with no inherited admission.

## What it does not earn

This does not claim:

- biological or memetic fitness;
- recommendation of descendants;
- automatic reproduction;
- truth preservation across descendants;
- licensing inheritance;
- source endorsement;
- automatic admission in later worlds;
- population-level cultural modeling;
- R11 checkpoint semantics.
