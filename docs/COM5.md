# COM⁵ — The reLATTE Metabolism

**Status:** architectural projection / non-normative  
**Date:** 2026-10-01  
**Promotion:** no protocol-schema promotion

> **The relation is the block. COM⁵ is the metabolism.**

reLATTE already has a structural grammar for attributable crossings, receiver-local admission, receipts, residuals, and reconstructible histories.

COM⁵ names a different axis: what a living ecology does with those structures over time.

```text
COMPOST
   ↓
COMPOSE
   ↓
COMPUTE
   ↓
COMMUTE
   ↓
COMMUNE
   ↓
COMPOST
   ↺
```

The five terms are **roles in a metabolic projection**, not mandatory lifecycle states.

A particular may be compost for one process, composition material for another, an input to computation in a third, commuting across a boundary, and participating in a commune elsewhere at the same time.

```text
COM5 PROJECTION != AUTHORITY
ROLE != ESSENCE
ROLE != GLOBAL STATE
```

---

## 1. COMPOST — history remains fertile

Compost is retained substrate whose primary moment may be over without its future usefulness being exhausted.

Examples include:

- residuals;
- refusals;
- stale cuts;
- failed seams;
- scars;
- superseded projections;
- abandoned branches;
- old receipts;
- partial provenance;
- spent material;
- unresolved questions.

```text
COMPOST != GARBAGE
COMPOST != CANON
COMPOST != AUTHORITY
RESIDUAL != FAILURE
CONSUMED != ERASED FROM HISTORY
```

Compost does not mean "everything must be reused."

It means prior material may remain addressable enough to participate in future composition without being silently promoted into truth or authority.

---

## 2. COMPOSE — relation makes possibility

Composition places particulars into a declared relation without requiring their merger.

```text
A     B     C
 \    |    /
   COMPOSE
      ↓
   relation R
```

The relation may be a crossing, merge, split, transform, comparison, room, organ composition, book, song, executable plan, or another typed structure.

```text
COMPOSITION != MERGER
COMPOSITION != OWNERSHIP
COMPOSITION != ADMISSION
RELATION != SOURCE COLLAPSE
```

A composition may propose a structure.

It does not inherit authority over its sources merely because it names them.

---

## 3. COMPUTE — admitted relation produces consequence

Compute is broader than "run code."

Within reLATTE it means applying declared local law to admitted inputs and producing attributable consequence.

```text
crossing / composition
        ↓
      RECEIVE
        ↓
       HOLD
        ↓
  owner-local law
        ↓
 ADMIT | REFUSE | RETURN
        ↓
    consequence
```

```text
COMPUTATION != AUTHORITY
VALID PROGRAM != AUTHORIZED EXECUTION
SAME INPUT != REQUIRED SAME CONSEQUENCE
```

Two sovereign receivers may verify the same crossing and lawfully produce different consequences.

That divergence is not necessarily failure.

---

## 4. COMMUTE — preserve the road without forcing the destination

Commute carries two useful meanings.

First: a crossing moves between places, worlds, media, carriers, or domains.

Second: in mathematics, a diagram commutes when different paths preserve a relevant relation or result.

reLATTE should use the second meaning narrowly.

A crossing may travel by:

- cloud relay;
- peer mesh;
- Git;
- removable media;
- local copy;
- another replaceable carrier.

If each route preserves the declared crossing invariants, the **road may commute**.

Candidate invariant surface:

```text
crossing identity
signature validity
declared ancestry
addressed payload bytes
declared protocol fields
```

But semantic consequence remains receiver-local.

```text
TRANSPORT COMMUTATION
!=
SEMANTIC UNIFORMITY

SAME CROSSING
→ World B: ADMIT
→ World C: HOLD
→ World D: REFUSE
```

Therefore:

> **The road may commute while the worlds diverge.**

Transport can preserve what was sent without dictating what a sovereign receiver must make of it.

---

## 5. COMMUNE — shared participation without absorption

Commune is relation becoming shared life.

A commune may be a group, room, project, gathering, network, band, institution, collaborative artifact, or other shared particular.

```text
A ─┐
B ─┼─ participate → shared particular/history
C ─┘

while

A != group
B != group
C != group
```

The group/admin/cloud research already supplies the constitutional guardrails:

```text
GROUP != MEMBER
MEMBERSHIP != IDENTITY
ADMINISTRATION != SOVEREIGNTY
CLOUD COORDINATION != LOCAL ADMISSION
```

Candidate compression:

> **Coordination may enable communion without manufacturing sovereignty.**

Participation may change capacity, culture, memory, susceptibility, or future relations.

It need not erase the participating particulars.

```text
COMMUNE != ABSORPTION
SHARED HISTORY != SHARED SOVEREIGN INTERIOR
```

---

## 6. The return: communion produces compost

A living commune creates remains:

- conversations;
- artifacts;
- forks;
- mistakes;
- receipts;
- refusals;
- derivatives;
- traditions;
- memories;
- unresolved differences;
- abandoned possibilities.

Those remains may become future substrate.

```text
COMMUNE
   ↓
RESIDUE
   ↓
COMPOST
   ↓
NEW COMPOSITION
```

The goal is not permanent closure.

The goal is enough retained structure for future life to remain possible without falsifying the past.

---

## 7. COM⁵ is orthogonal to protocol state

Do not add this:

```json
{
  "com5_stage": "COMMUTE"
}
```

That would falsely imply one global lifecycle position.

Prefer a derived view:

```text
particular Q
  ├─ COMPOST in process α
  ├─ COMPOSE input in process β
  └─ COMMUNE participant in process γ
```

A COM⁵ renderer may summarize attributable observations or relations.

It must not silently promote the projection into protocol truth.

```text
OBSERVATION != ESSENCE
PROJECTION != AUTHORITY
RENDERING != AUTHORITY
```

## 8. Donor fit

COM⁵ does not absorb donor projects. It provides a lens over pressures already present across them.

| Role | Existing pressure |
| --- | --- |
| COMPOST | TranchNode residue, SEAMforge failures, Iron Lung scars, Daily Slice memory, coffee residuals |
| COMPOSE | National Treasure, Free Graph/BODY, organ composition, lineage operators |
| COMPUTE | Band Runtime admission, owner-local execution, signed receipts |
| COMMUTE | crossing protocol, relay/mirror transport, canonical identity, federation |

The fit is descriptive.

```text
FIT != OWNERSHIP
RESEMBLANCE != ANCESTRY
METABOLIC ROLE != PROTOCOL PROMOTION
```

---

## 9. Executable boundary

The first executable COM⁵ seam is deliberately small.

It may:

- accept explicitly supplied metabolic observations;
- allow one subject to occupy multiple roles;
- group observations in canonical COM⁵ display order;
- render a human-readable trace.

It may not:

- infer truth from role labels;
- mutate crossing or receipt identity;
- decide admission;
- authorize execution;
- infer source ancestry;
- claim a global lifecycle state.

This makes the executable surface an **observability instrument**, not a new sovereign runtime.

```text
COMPOST != GARBAGE
COMPOSE != MERGER
```

And:
