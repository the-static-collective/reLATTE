# ANY INVITATION CARTRIDGE 001

**Intent:** The invitation relation is domain-neutral. A volunteer performance, a donated tool, an unanswered question, a surplus meal, a repair, a ride or a prospective world can each become a voluntary invitation. A receiving person can act, offer, question, pass, fork or refuse without incurring a social penalty.

This inert, declarative specimen is recorded at `cartridges/any-invitation-001.json` and consumed by JAR;VIS as a proposal-only input. It is NOT a cryptographically sealed reLATTE crossing, a binding capability grant, an identity claim, consent, a server-side social graph or a notification service.

## Contract

- **Origin:** a particular self-declared, public-safe possibility. Its source owner retains the right to edit or withdraw *their own future posting*, but cannot remotely erase a screenshot or a voluntarily reshared copy.
- **Time:** NOW preferred, not assumed; SOON and OPEN remain valid.
- **Carrying:** a portable public link fragment. Explicit human share only; no automatic fan-out or discovery of contacts.
- **Receiving:** accept for local consideration, ask, offer, fork, pass, decline. No button itself transports goods, posts media, commits other people, or creates authority.
- **Descendants:** linked forks have self-declared parentage. No verified attribution until separately witnessed, signed and checked.
- **Return:** in the current browser specimen, responses are local artifacts for explicit person-to-person sharing. A real private return inbox with receipt, consent and withdrawal remains future work.
- **Learning:** no hidden engagement optimization. Optional explicit local feedback may reorder a user's action deck within bounded values, never automatically contact anyone.

The full webZ proof is `docs/ANY-INVITATION-CHAIN-001.md` in its own repository. JAR;VIS owns its strict action-planner consumer. reLATTE owns only this portable contract specimen and no participant.

**Invariant:** NOW FIRST != FORCE NOW. INVITE != ENROLL. FORK != AUTHORITY. RETURN PREPARED != RETURN RECEIVED.
