# Super Relatte 001

Super Relatte is the composition branch for the reLATTE family.

It does **not** flatten the branches. It preserves the laws, receipts, and
lineage of the mutations while providing one executable surface on top of the
current mainline.

## Composition rule

**Current main is the authority-bearing substrate. Diverged branches are organ
donors, not replacement worlds.**

The integration therefore keeps the current protocol, receiver, ecology,
mirror/store/serve, mortality, porch, identity, pulse, organ, field,
descendant, checkpoint, transport, and sovereign-node work from `main`, then
grafts the missing late room lineage.

## Recovered executable lineage

The branch `feat/room-return-request` is the apex descendant of:

1. `feat/com5-metabolism`
2. `feat/web5-five-door-room`
3. `feat/browse-metabolism`
4. `feat/walkable-provenance`
5. `feat/reentry-witness`
6. `feat/enterable-particular`
7. `feat/room-return-request`

Those mutations become one sequence:

`signed history -> COM5 observations -> five-door room -> verified neighborhood
-> attributable traversal -> re-entry witness -> enterable particular ->
receiver-reverified room return`

## Two COM5 layers

Super Relatte intentionally carries both:

- **COM5 capsule:** portable grammar with explicit origin, five stages,
  receiver policy, return address, and receipt semantics.
- **COM5 projection:** a local, non-authoritative five-door view over explicit
  observations.

They are complementary. The capsule is what may travel; the projection is how
a receiver may look.

## Orthogonal branch recovery

Super Relatte also recovers non-apex material that had not reached current
`main`:

- National Coffee Day: lineage operators and the cup-as-graph slice.
- Groups / Administration / Cloud: source packet and
  `the-group-does-not-own-the-member` slice.
- R3 sovereign receiver: plan and design specification.

README-era snapshots from those branches are not allowed to overwrite the
newer mainline README.

## Super surface

`buildSuperRelatteView(subject, records, encounterPath?)` returns:

- the verified metabolic neighborhood;
- the enterable-particular projection;
- explicit composition semantics;
- explicit non-authority boundaries.

The view does not mutate source history and does not turn room navigation into
protocol authority.

## Governing non-collapse

- PROJECTION != AUTHORITY
- DOOR != CROSSING
- RECOMMENDATION != SELECTION
- OMISSION != NONEXISTENCE
- REENTRY != IDENTITY REPLACEMENT
- RECEIVER ENCOUNTER != SOURCE IDENTITY

Super Relatte is therefore not "one branch wins."

It is: **the branches become addressable organs in one body without losing
their receipts.**
