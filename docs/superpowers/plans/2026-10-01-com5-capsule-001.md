> One attributable creative grammar can cross reLATTE unchanged, be admitted by one sovereign receiver and refused by another, and preserve both outcomes without transferring source authority.

1. Define a content-addressed COM⁵ capsule.

3. Bind origin attribution and non-authority declarations into capsule identity.
4. Wrap the capsule as a reLATTE crossing.
5. Evaluate the same crossing against two synthetic receiver policies.
6. Emit independent signed receipts.
7. Prove ADMIT and REFUSE can coexist over one crossing ID.
8. Prove mutation of attribution/non-authority changes capsule identity.
9. Refuse to claim durable R3 receiver completion.

## Test-first pressure

Required tests:

- stage reordering is rejected;
- origin/non-authority mutation changes capsule ID;
- sealed capsule verifies;
- one signed crossing binds the capsule ID;
- two receiver policies see the same crossing ID;
- performance specimen admits;
- archive specimen refuses;
- refusal has semantic_effect = none;
- receipts are independently signed and verifiable;
- COMMUNE means shared addressability, not equal disposition.

Persistence, networking, duplicate delivery, and restart reconstruction belong to the actual R3 implementation rather than this specimen.
