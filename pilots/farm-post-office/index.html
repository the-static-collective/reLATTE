The idea

Connect systems. Keep control.

We aren't asking you to replace your storefront or email provider. The goal is automatic, permission-aware communication between them, even when they belong to different organizations.

Your email tool

The appropriate action

Update a contact, apply an unsubscribe, or send only when your rules allow it.

Important distinction: Buying vegetables does not automatically subscribe someone to marketing emails. The bridge has to respect opt-ins, unsubscribes, and the farmer's own rules.

First farm pilot

Start with one small useful connection.

Our first proposed trial is simple: one authorized storefront change automatically updates one appropriate record in your chosen email system, with a receipt you can inspect. Start with synthetic data or a few agreed-upon test records, not your entire customer list.

What we'd work out together

Which storefront are you using?

For example, a co-op e-commerce service, a farm shop, or a CSA platform.

What handles your emails?

Could be a newsletter provider, a CRM, or email marketing inside your website.

What would help first?

New contacts, CSA segments, fulfillment notices, or preventing unsubscribed people from receiving mail.

Who approves the connection?

You choose the rules and grant access only if you decide to proceed. No password sharing.

Experimental prototype · No cost or commitment to explore fit · Scope, any ongoing costs, and responsibilities would be agreed before real integration.
