NOW ROUTE is a separate, geographically limited opt-in service. It does not import these packets or verify their signatures. Separately authorize any live offer or need there. No production database writes or public posts occur in this offline desk.

What a crossing proves—and what it cannot

Portable cartridges use browser-generated P-256 signatures to check that each packet was signed by the holder of a session private key and later acknowledgments refer to the same message. Keys identify a session—not a legally verified person. HOLD/ADMIT/REFUSE govern permission to continue evaluating a specific small test; they never reserve or transfer property or prove completed delivery. Keep the browser tab open through the handshake or restart with a new session key.
