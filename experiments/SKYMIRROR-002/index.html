OFFLINE OPTICAL EXPERIMENT — NOT A SATELLITE LINK

Screen transmitter

Enter a tiny message. Place the mirror where the second phone can see this screen's reflection. Keep this page open and the screen unobstructed.

2 bytes · approx 70 sec

The screen flashes black and white (around 1–2 brightness transitions per second). Not suitable for photosensitive viewers. Lower ambient light or turn up manual screen brightness if needed. Do not direct lasers at the sky, people or aircraft.

Camera receiver

On phone B, tap Start Camera, point the rear camera at the mirror image of phone A, then start the transmitter on phone A. Keep both phones still until capture completes.
