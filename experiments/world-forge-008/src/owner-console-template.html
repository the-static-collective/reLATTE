WORLD-FORGE-008 is a local, experimental authorization ceremony. This page does not contact any server. No button here grants legal power, logs in to GitHub, merges code, or admits a foreign cartridge.

Proof is not identity

Your browser can create a nonexportable P-256 signing key and prove that the same local key signed a project delegation. It cannot establish your real-world identity or convert an AI assistant into a legal agent. You decide when and with whom to share each signed file.
